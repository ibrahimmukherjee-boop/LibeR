LibeR bundled-runtime corresponding source
===========================================

The LibeR installer redistributes R and open-source R packages in binary form.
The exact corresponding source archives, versions, origins, and SHA-256
checksums are provided in the companion
`LibeR-<version>-corresponding-source.zip` archive. Research-profile
installations also include this material under `developer\sources`.

For components licensed under the GNU General Public License, the distributor
offers to provide the same machine-readable corresponding source for at least
three years after the last distribution of the applicable installer, for no
more than the reasonable physical cost of providing it. Requests can be made
through https://github.com/svdijkman/LibeR/issues.

Each component remains subject to its own licence. Installed package metadata,
licence files, provenance records, and the generated `sources.csv` manifest
identify the redistributed versions. This notice does not replace the licence
texts shipped with R or individual packages.
